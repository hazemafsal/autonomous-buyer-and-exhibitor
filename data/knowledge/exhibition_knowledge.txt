# BUYER-EXHIBITOR MATCHING RULES

A strong buyer-exhibitor match should consider:

1. Industry compatibility.
2. Product and requirement compatibility.
3. Budget compatibility.
4. Geographic compatibility.
5. Purchase urgency.
6. Enterprise scale.
7. Technology compatibility.
8. Business expansion potential.

The system should prioritize the buyer's actual business requirements rather than superficial keyword similarity.

A buyer should receive a high match score when the exhibitor can realistically provide the products, technologies or services required by the buyer.

Budget compatibility should consider whether the buyer's budget falls within or reasonably overlaps the exhibitor's typical project budget.

Geographic compatibility should prioritize exhibitors operating in the buyer's target location.

Urgency compatibility should prioritize exhibitors capable of supporting the buyer's expected purchasing timeline.
